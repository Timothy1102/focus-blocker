# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-11-09_

Focus Blocker does not collect, transmit or share any data.

## What is stored

The only data the extension keeps is the list of websites you chose to block, together with each site's on/off state. It is saved in your browser's local extension storage (`chrome.storage.local`) on your own device. It is not synced to your Google account and is removed when you uninstall the extension.

## What is not collected

- No browsing history, page content or visited URLs are recorded.
- No personal information, accounts or identifiers are used.
- No analytics, telemetry, advertising or crash reporting.
- No network requests are made by the extension, and no remote code is loaded.

## Permissions

The extension asks for broad site access only so that Chrome can redirect the sites **you** choose to its "blocked" page. Chrome applies those redirect rules itself. The extension reads the address of a tab only to offer "Block current site" and to redirect tabs that are already open on a site you just blocked. Nothing is sent anywhere. The [README](README.md#permissions) explains each permission.
